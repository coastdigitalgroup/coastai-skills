# B2B Champion Enablement Audit Checklist & Optimization Template

Use this comprehensive checklist to audit your B2B website, post-demo workflows, and sales enablement assets. This template identifies internal buying friction and guides the creation of self-serve champion toolkits.

---

## 1. Post-Demo Confirmation & Buyer Hub Readiness

- [ ] **Immediate Post-Booking Redirection:**
  - [ ] Are demo bookers immediately redirected to a dedicated Buyer Hub page (e.g., `/buyer-hub` or `/champions`) rather than a generic "Thanks, check your calendar" screen?
  - [ ] Does the confirmation page provide a single shareable link for the evaluator to forward to their team?
- [ ] **Automated Post-Demo Email Kit:**
  - [ ] Is an automated follow-up email dispatched within 1 hour containing an editable pitch deck link and executive one-pager?
  - [ ] Does the email include pre-written Slack/Email text for the champion to copy-paste to their leadership team?
- [ ] **Multi-Stakeholder Portal Access:**
  - [ ] Can the champion invite additional team members or stakeholders directly to the digital evaluation workspace?

---

## 2. Executive & CFO Enablement Assets

- [ ] **Executive One-Pager (1-Page PDF / Web Card):**
  - [ ] Does the asset summarize the **Business Problem**, **Proposed Solution**, and **Quantified ROI** without technical jargon?
  - [ ] Is the investment cost framed against the **Cost of Inaction** (monthly loss from status quo)?
  - [ ] Is the asset ungated and instantly downloadable without filling out additional lead forms?
- [ ] **Customizable Pitch Deck (Google Slides / PowerPoint):**
  - [ ] Is the presentation editable so the champion can insert their own logo, team size, and internal company goals?
  - [ ] Does it include a slide comparing tested alternatives (Status Quo vs. Legacy Competitors vs. Proposed Vendor)?
  - [ ] Does it include an explicit 30-60-90 day onboarding and implementation timeline?
- [ ] **Interactive Business Case & ROI Calculator:**
  - [ ] Is there an interactive ROI calculator on the website or buyer hub requiring <5 input fields?
  - [ ] Does the calculator automatically calculate **Annual Financial Savings ($)**, **Payback Period (Months)**, and **3-Year ROI (%)**?
  - [ ] Can the champion export the calculated results as a 1-page PDF Executive Memo addressed to their CFO/VP?

---

## 3. CISO, IT Security & Legal Compliance Fast-Track

- [ ] **Public Trust & Security Portal (`/security` or `/trust`):**
  - [ ] Are compliance certifications (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR, CCPA) prominently displayed with current validation badges?
  - [ ] Is there an active system uptime/status link (e.g., status.company.com)?
  - [ ] Is data encryption guidance (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit) clearly detailed?
- [ ] **Self-Serve Security Review Packet:**
  - [ ] Is a completed standard CAIQ or SIG Lite security questionnaire available for immediate download?
  - [ ] Are network architecture and data flow diagrams included in the download packet?
  - [ ] Is a standard Data Processing Agreement (DPA) available for legal review?
- [ ] **Instant Click-Through NDA:**
  - [ ] If SOC 2 Type II reports require an NDA, is there a 1-click web NDA execution flow rather than requiring manual legal signature exchange?

---

## 4. Competitive Evaluation & Scorecards

- [ ] **Vendor Comparison Scorecards:**
  - [ ] Is there an objective feature and business capability comparison matrix evaluating your product against main competitors and manual spreadsheets?
  - [ ] Are key decision criteria prioritized by business impact (e.g., Time-to-Deploy, Admin Overhead, Integration Depth)?
- [ ] **Migration & Integration Roadmaps:**
  - [ ] Does the website provide clear technical integration guides for existing tech stack tools (e.g., Salesforce, Slack, AWS, Snowflake)?
  - [ ] Is there a clear migration guide showing how easy it is to transition from legacy tools?

## 6. Optimization Audit Scoring & Action Plan

- **20–25 Checkbox Items Checked:** **Optimized (Class Leader).** Your website acts as a 24/7 sales enablement engine for champions.
- **12–19 Checkbox Items Checked:** **Moderate Friction.** Champions are doing heavy lifting manually. Deploy missing CFO memos and security packets.
- **<12 Checkbox Items Checked:** **Severe Friction (High Deal Stall Risk).** Deals rely entirely on sales rep calls. Immediate deployment of `/buyer-hub` required.
