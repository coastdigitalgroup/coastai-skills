Can I change or cancel my plan at any time?

Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription directly from your billing settings at any time without penalty or hidden cancellation fees.

If you cancel, your account features will remain active until the end of your current billing period.

Is my organization's data encrypted at rest and in transit?

All customer data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 bit encryption. We maintain SOC 2 Type II compliance and ISO 27001 certification.

What are the API rate limits for developers?

Standard Growth plans include 1,000 requests per minute per API key. Enterprise plans offer custom throttles up to 50,000 requests per minute with dedicated infrastructure.

How does per-seat pricing work for team members?

You are billed only for active team members. When you add a new seat mid-cycle, we automatically calculate a prorated charge for the remaining days in your billing period.
