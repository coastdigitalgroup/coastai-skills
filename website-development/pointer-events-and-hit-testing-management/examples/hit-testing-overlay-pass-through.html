Pointer Events & Hit-Testing Management

Demonstration of pass-through overlays (`pointer-events: none`), continuous pointer capture (`setPointerCapture`), and hit-box expansion.

1. Pass-Through HUD Overlay

The transparent overlay has pointer-events: none, allowing clicks to reach the underlying button, while the "HUD Settings" button re-enables interaction with pointer-events: auto.

2. Continuous Pointer Capture Slider

Click and drag the thumb handle. Notice that even if your mouse/finger leaves the handle bounds, setPointerCapture() keeps tracking movement continuously.

A small 18px icon button expanded to 48px target size using ::before pseudo-element hit area without changing layout flow.
